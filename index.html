HTML On The Best Movie I Have Seen Lately

The best movie I have seen lately was My Neighbour Totoro, an animated Japanese film by director Hayao Miyazaki from Studio Ghibli. I have rewatched the film a handful of times at this point. It's an adorable storyline, and the beautiful graphics make it an easy film to watch anytime I want something familiar and comforting.

The film was originally released in 1988 and is one of many widely recognized films created by Hayao Miyazaki and the Studio Ghibli animation studio.
